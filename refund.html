Refund Policy

Last updated October 1, 2026

14-day money-back guarantee. If Typewave Pro isn't right for you, email us within 14 days of paying and we'll refund the full amount, including tax.

How to ask for a refund

Email support@fenwicklabs.app from the Google account you used to buy Pro, or include your PayPal / Razorpay payment ID. You don't need to give a reason, though we'd love to know what we could do better.

How long it takes

We process refunds within 3 business days. PayPal refunds usually arrive right away; card refunds take 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

What happens to Pro

A refund removes the Pro time that payment bought. Your snippets stay on your computer, and Typewave continues on the free plan.

No surprise renewals

Typewave never charges you automatically. Monthly and Yearly plans simply end on the date shown in your account, so there's nothing to cancel.

After 14 days

We look at every request fairly. If something went wrong, such as a double charge or a feature that doesn't work for you, write to us and we'll make it right.

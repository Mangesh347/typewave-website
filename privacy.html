Privacy Policy

Last updated October 1, 2026

The short version: your snippets and what you type stay in your browser. Typewave's AI runs on your own computer. We only know who you are if you sign in with Google to buy Pro, and we never sell data.

Who we are

Typewave is a Chrome extension and website made by Fenwick Labs ("we"). Questions about privacy: support@fenwicklabs.app.

What stays on your computer

  • Your snippets, folders, and settings are stored in Chrome's local storage on your computer.
  • What you type on web pages is checked only inside your browser, only to spot a snippet shortcut you just typed. Typewave doesn't record, store, or send what you type. It never runs in password fields.
  • Usage counts (how many times each snippet was used, characters saved per day) are kept locally to power the Insights page.
  • AI features use Gemini Nano, the model built into Chrome, which runs on your device. Text you give the AI Writer or rewrite isn't sent to us or to any AI provider.
  • Clipboard is read only when a snippet you insert contains the {clipboard} placeholder.

Sync (Pro, optional)

If you turn on sync, Typewave copies your snippets into Chrome's own sync storage, tied to the Google account you're signed in to in Chrome. Google handles that sync under Google's Privacy Policy. Typewave's servers never receive your snippets.

Uninstall survey

If you remove Typewave, we open a short, optional survey. Answers are stored without any identifier: just the reason, your note, and the extension version.

Who we share data with

Only the processors that run the service: Supabase (database and sign-in), Vercel (website hosting), PayPal and Razorpay (payments), and Google (sign-in). We don't sell personal information, show ads, or use tracking pixels.

Chrome Web Store user data policy

Typewave's use of information follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide Typewave's single purpose: inserting your saved text where you type.

Keeping and deleting data

Local data is deleted when you remove the extension or use Settings → Delete all. To delete your account and purchase records (except what tax law requires us to keep), email support@fenwicklabs.app from the address you signed in with. We reply within 30 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR in the EU/UK or CCPA in California), you can ask to see, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and object to processing. Email us and we'll help.

Children

Typewave isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Significant changes will be announced in the extension's release notes.

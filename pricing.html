Billing questions

Does it renew automatically?

No. Monthly gives you 30 days and Yearly gives you 365 days. We show the end date, and you can buy more time whenever you like. Buying again adds time to the end of your current plan.

What's included in Lifetime?

Every Pro feature, now and in future updates, for a single payment.

Which currencies and payment methods?

Prices show in USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, CAD, or INR depending on where you are. PayPal works worldwide. Cards are processed by Razorpay; buyers in India can also use UPI.

Is tax included?

Prices are shown before tax. If your country charges VAT or GST on digital services, it's added as its own line at checkout before you pay.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 14 days of paying, no questions asked. See the refund policy.

How do I use Pro on another computer?

Install Typewave there, open Settings → Account, and sign in with the same Google account. Turn on Sync to bring your snippets along.
